* A useful identity
The first $n$ positive integers have a simple sum:

$ sum_(k=1)^n k = (n(n+1))/2 $

A longer display equation may use delimiters on their own lines:

$
  integral_0^1 x dif x = 1/2
$

* A short plan
- State the identity clearly.
- Check a small example.
- Explain why the result is useful.

1. For $n=3$, the left side is $1+2+3=6$.
2. The right side is $(3(3+1))/2=6$.
3. The two expressions agree.
